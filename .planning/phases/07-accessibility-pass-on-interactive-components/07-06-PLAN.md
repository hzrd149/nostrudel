Convert the article views and the link cards (D-16 wave 2, after the shared components): the
article page stops forcing a second `main` role, its author block and content block become native
`footer` and `article`, its header loses the wrong heading role, article tags become a native list,

duplicate it. D-08 deletes the header's heading role instead of inventing an `aria-level` for a
second heading. D-20 keeps the `Box as="header"` inside the article so it is scoped to it. D-13
keeps every element visually identical.

commits and one `fix(07-06)` commit (D-17).

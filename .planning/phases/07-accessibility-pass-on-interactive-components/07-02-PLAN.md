<objective>
Convert the layout and navigation shared components that every view inherits (D-16 wave 1):
VerticalPageLayout renders a native `main`, the redundant roles on the side nav and button groups
are deleted, the invoice modal body becomes a named `section`, the page-wide live region is removed,
and the mobile drawer becomes a named dialog containing a `nav`.

Purpose: D-06 replaces landmark roles with native elements; D-18 deletes the two `ButtonGroup`
group roles because Chakra already renders them; D-04 removes the page-wide `aria-live` that makes
screen readers announce every content change; D-19 names the drawer dialog. The planner found that
two creation views pass a form element into VerticalPageLayout, which would override the new `main`
element and drop their only `main` landmark, so they nest their form inside the layout instead.

Output: 6 bucket-F findings cleared (rows 1-6 of 07-BASELINE.md) in three commits: two mechanical
`refactor(07-02)` commits and one user-noticeable `fix(07-02)` commit (D-17).
</objective>

- Why the two form views change (planner finding, not in RESEARCH): today
  `<VerticalPageLayout as="form">` yields a form element whose explicit main role makes it the page's
  main landmark. Once the inner `Flex` sets the element with `as` and drops the role, the caller's
  element override wins (props spread last) and those pages would have no `main` at all. Nesting the
  form keeps one `main` per page (UI-SPEC "Landmark structure") with identical layout: the form `Flex`
  repeats `direction="column" gap="2"` and stretches to full width inside the column. This was
  type-checked and scanned in a scratch worktree during planning (tsc clean, no new diagnostic).

<objective>
Clear the event tools' four directives and dispose of the Vertex cast as the user ruled: fix the
event-console relative-time unit quirk with a tested typed parser (D-18, own commit), remove the
two event-publisher directives with real types (D-12), and keep Vertex's cast behind a reasoned
rule-scoped ignore (D-17).

Purpose: the event-console regex matches unit letters case-insensitively but passed the raw letter
to dayjs, so `n-5H`, `W`, `S`, `D` meant milliseconds and `M` meant months. D-18 rules that
uppercase means the same as lowercase, as the help modal documents, and that this deliberate
behavior change gets its own commit and test. D-17 rules that Vertex is slated for replacement by
the Open-Ranking protocol API, so its stale sort-method values are not corrected now.

Output: 5 bucket-E findings resolved (4 fixed, 1 behind a rule-scoped ignore) and one new test file.
</objective>
